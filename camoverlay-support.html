CamOverlay Support

CamOverlay keeps your camera in a small floating window that stays on top of every app — even full-screen ones. Built for screen recordings, tutorials, live streams and demos.

Getting started

CamOverlay lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon or main window. After launching, look for the camera-shaped icon in the menu bar at the top-right of your screen — every control is in that menu. On first launch macOS asks for camera permission; choose Allow. The camera window then appears and can be dragged anywhere on screen.

What you can adjust

  • Shape — rectangle, square, circle, squircle, hexagon, star, heart, a corner shape that snaps to the nearest screen corner, and two 3D perspective tilts
  • Border — thickness slider plus eight colours
  • Filter — ten live looks including Mono, Noir, Sepia, Chrome and Comic
  • Opacity, corner radius and size — small, medium or large
  • Camera — pick any connected camera, including an iPhone via Continuity Camera
  • Mirror mode and Always on Top toggles, plus Reset Position

Troubleshooting

The window is black or empty. Camera permission was probably denied. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, enable CamOverlay, then restart the app.

I can't find the window. Choose Reset Position from the menu bar icon to bring it back to the bottom-right corner.

My camera isn't listed. Make sure it is connected and not in use by another app in exclusive mode, then reopen the Camera submenu.
