BlockPop! Support

BlockPop! is a clean, ad-free block puzzle for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro.

How to play

Drag blocks from your hand onto the board. Fill a full row or column to clear it and score points. Clear several lines at once for combo bonuses. When no piece fits, the run ends. Modes: Classic (8x8 endless), Chaos (10x10, 5-piece hand), and Adventure (level challenges).

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center to appear on leaderboards and earn achievements. It is optional — the game is fully playable without it.
