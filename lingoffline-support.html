Frequently asked questions

Does the app really work offline?

Yes. All 35 languages and every sentence are bundled inside the app. No internet connection is ever required.

How do I change my languages?

Tap the language icon in the top-right corner of the home screen, then pick the language you speak and the language you want to say things in. You can swap them any time.

The Listen button is silent for some languages.

Pronunciation uses the voices installed on your iPhone. If a language stays silent, open iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices and download a voice for that language.

How do I copy a sentence?

Open any sentence and tap Copy under either language. The text is placed on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

What happened to İngilizce Rehberi?

İngilizce Rehberi grew up: it is now Lingoffline and works between any two of 35 languages — Turkish→English included, exactly as before.
