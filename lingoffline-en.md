# Lang Offline Privacy Policy

*Effective date: June 26, 2026*

Lang Offline does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not collect personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, contact information, location data, photos, audio, or user content.

Lang Offline works offline. Your language choices and saved phrases are stored only on your device. The app does not require an account and does not send app activity to a server.

Lang Offline uses the system text-to-speech engine to pronounce phrases and the system clipboard when you copy a phrase. These actions happen on your device. Installing an optional system voice is handled by iOS.

Lang Offline does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

For support, see [Lang Offline Support](lingoffline-support.html).
