Overview

BlockPop! is a block puzzle game for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro, developed by Fırat Yalavuz. You drag colorful blocks onto a board, clear lines, and chase combos across Classic, Chaos, and Adventure modes. This policy explains how BlockPop! handles information.

Data We Do Not Collect

BlockPop! does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app:

  • Does not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera
  • Does not track your usage or behavior
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs
  • Does not create its own account or require a sign-in
  • Does not use cookies or tracking technologies

How the App Works

BlockPop! runs entirely on your device. Your high scores, Adventure progress, and settings are stored locally on the device only, using the system's standard local storage (UserDefaults). Deleting the app removes this data.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center, BlockPop! submits your scores to leaderboards and reports achievements through Game Center so you can compare progress with other players. This is an optional Apple feature — the game is fully playable without it. Any information handled through Game Center is processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by the developer. BlockPop! does not receive or store other players' personal information.

Third-Party Services

Aside from Apple's own Game Center, BlockPop! does not integrate with any third-party services, backends, authentication providers, payment processors, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

BlockPop! does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
