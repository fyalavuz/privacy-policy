Overview

AR Camera PoC is an iOS camera application prototype developed by Fırat Yalavuz. It is designed to create photos and videos with selected augmented reality effects. This policy explains how information is handled when the app uses the camera, microphone, media library, and Camera Kit.

Your Media and On-device Processing

Camera imagery and video audio are processed on the device to provide the app experience. The app does not automatically upload your photos or videos to a server operated by Fırat Yalavuz.

  • Your photos and videos are not sent to Photos or another app unless you explicitly choose to save or share them.
  • The app may create temporary local files while recording; those files are cleaned up after a recording is completed or cancelled.
  • Media you save to Photos is managed by Apple under your device settings and Apple account.

Permissions

  • Camera: to display the live camera, apply AR effects, and capture photos or videos.
  • Microphone: to record audio with a video.
  • Photos: only to save a photo or video that you choose to add to your library.

You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings. If you decline a permission, the related feature may not be available.

Snap Camera Kit and Lenses

The app uses Snap Camera Kit SDK to run AR Lenses. Lenses may be downloaded while you have an internet connection and later used from the device cache. Cached Lens assets may be removed by the operating system.

During normal operation, Snap may collect limited technical data not linked to you, such as product-interaction, crash, and performance diagnostics. This processing is governed by Snap's own policies. For more information, see the Snap Privacy Policy and Snap Terms of Service.

Data We Do Not Collect

In this PoC version, the app does not operate user accounts, advertising, personalized ad profiles, or cloud storage for user media. App analytics events do not include photos, videos, or face imagery.

Third Parties and Sharing

When you open the system share sheet, the privacy policy of the app or service you choose applies. AR Lens services are provided by Snap Camera Kit. This policy does not cover the data practices of Apple, Snap, or a third-party app you choose to use.

Children's Privacy

AR Camera PoC is not designed for children. If a version of the app is released for children, its age, consent, and content policies will be updated separately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or service providers. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
